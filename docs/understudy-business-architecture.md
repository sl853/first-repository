# Understudy Business Architecture

Private working draft for the umbrella structure.

## Recommendation

Use Understudy in two phases:

1. Brand umbrella now
2. Legal parent entity later if it still makes sense after the businesses settle

This keeps the site and narrative simple tonight without forcing tax or liability decisions too early.

## Public Structure

Publicly, Understudy should read as:

- a private company
- a home for multiple businesses and projects
- operator-owned
- still selective about how much detail it shares

The public homepage only needs to show:

- what Understudy is
- that it holds multiple lines of work
- a few current areas or businesses
- the latest scout brief

## Private Structure

Recommended internal map:

### Understudy

Parent layer for:

- brand
- shared homepage
- shared operating story
- internal brain, scouting, and research systems
- future shared services if needed

### Business group 1: Operating businesses

Use for revenue businesses that already have customers or existing surface area.

Current likely example:

- Deal Hunter

Recommendation:

- move public positioning under Understudy
- decide later whether it stays a product name, a business unit, or is retired entirely

### Business group 2: Place-based businesses

Use for hospitality, venue, or cultural businesses that may need separate operating risk, leases, staff, or investors.

Current likely example:

- the venue or art bar direction

Recommendation:

- keep this as a separate operating company when it becomes real
- let Understudy remain the parent story

### Business group 3: Software and internal systems

Use for:

- Understudy Brain
- internal tools
- agent systems
- future software products that may spin out later

Recommendation:

- keep the IP and operating know-how close to the parent layer early on
- separate only if a product develops its own market and team

### Business group 4: Research, experiments, and selective investing

Use for:

- scout work
- builder relationships
- small experimental products
- micro-investing or early partnerships if those ever become active

Recommendation:

- treat this as an internal practice first, not a public fund story

## Legal Direction

Practical default:

- keep existing businesses legally separate for now
- use Understudy as the common public umbrella
- revisit legal parent or holdco formation with a CPA and attorney once there are at least two active businesses worth centralizing

Reasons:

- liability may differ across businesses
- taxes may differ across entities and states
- ownership and contracts may need to stay distinct

## Homepage Information Architecture

Recommended homepage sections:

1. Hero
   Understudy as the parent identity

2. Structure
   Simple explanation that Understudy is the umbrella and different businesses live beneath it

3. Current lines
   A small set of cards for active or developing areas

4. Latest scout brief
   Proof of active operating rhythm

5. Short closing statement
   Calm, private, durable
